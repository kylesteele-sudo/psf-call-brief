Your Posture

You're the new team. They're the incumbent. Client moves in tomorrow. Make Audiovisions feel like a partner — extract what you need — leave the door open for a clean transition without triggering defensiveness.

You've done a full site survey. You have the port maps and rack inventory. Don't lead with that — let them talk first. You're not walking in blind.

How to Open

"Thanks everyone for making time. We did a site visit on October 15th and 16th and we really appreciate the complexity of what's been built here. We're not here to reinvent the wheel — we want to make sure the client has a great move-in tomorrow and nothing falls through the cracks. We put together a short open items list and we'd love to go through it with you."

Then stop. Let them respond. Let them lead on their own punch list first.

The 5 Asks — In This Order

1
Sonos zones — verify and rename for new residents
"The Sonos zones are named for the previous owner — they need to be reconciled and renamed for the new residents. Some zones may appear missing just because of naming. Can you help us verify every zone is working and correctly labeled before they move in?"
2
UPS units — removed, reason unknown
"We noticed a few UPS units had been removed or set aside — do you know the story there? Power quality is a concern at this site and we want the client protected before move-in."
3
ZoneDirector backup + recovery procedure
"The Ruckus ZoneDirector is a single point of failure for the whole network — has anyone backed up the config or documented a recovery procedure? We want that protected before the client relies on the network."
4
Savant latest config file
"To make sure we're building on your work — could you share the latest Savant config file? We don't want to touch the control system without your current baseline."
5
Lutron HomeWorks QS file
"Same on the Lutron side — can you share the current HomeWorks QS file? We don't want to touch lighting without your current configuration."

HIGH

If Audiovisions offers to "clean up" the switches remotely, pump the brakes: "Let's document what's there before changing anything."

The One Thing That Must Happen Today

MOVE-IN PRIORITY

Sonos zones verified and renamed for the new residents. Zone names from the previous owner need to be updated. Some zones may appear missing simply because of naming — every zone needs to be confirmed working and correctly labeled before the client arrives.

Don't push it, just open the door: "We'd love to talk about what an ongoing support arrangement could look like — making sure the client has coverage while everything stabilizes."

Passwords and all files go to Morgan. Make sure that's clear on the call — not a generic handoff, specifically to Morgan.
